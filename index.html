Meet the team

Hi, we are .......

We're a small team of certified instructors who believe Pilates works best outdoors — on real grass, under open sky, moving as a group instead of alone in a studio mirror.

What started as one mat on a Gold Coast lawn has grown into a community that meets rain or shine, chasing the same feeling: strength that comes from slowing down.

Our Philosophy

Nourish. Move. Bloom

Nourish

We start from the ground up — breath, grounding, and attention to how your body actually feels before we ask it to move.

Move

Every class is controlled strength work — slow, deliberate movement that builds real stability, not just a sweat.

Bloom

Consistency compounds. Most of our members tell us the biggest shift wasn't physical — it was showing up for themselves.

What makes us different?

More than just a Pilates Class

Small groups, capped at ten, so instructors actually know your name and your form.

Every session is held outdoors, weather permitting, with a wet-weather studio nearby.

Mats and props are provided — just bring water and a towel.

No mirrors, no machines — just your body, your breath, and the grass beneath you.

MAT PILATES

The Pilates Garden

Mat Pilates is slow, controlled strength work done at ground level — no machines, just your body, breath, and the grass beneath you. The Pilates Garden brings it outdoors, in small groups, on Gold Coast lawns.

Classes

Sculpt

Slow, controlled reformer-style mat work that builds long, lean strength through the core and stabilising muscles.

Sweat

An up-tempo blend of Pilates and conditioning intervals designed to lift the heart rate without leaving the mat.

Stretch

A slower, breath-led class focused on mobility, release, and lengthening tight muscles after a busy week.

Sunset

Our signature golden-hour class on the lawn — a full-body flow as the Gold Coast sky turns pink and gold.

ABOUT OUR PILATES STYLE

Mat Pilates needs nothing but a mat and the ground beneath it. We take it outdoors, onto real grass, under real sky — because moving alongside nature settles the mind as much as it strengthens the body.
